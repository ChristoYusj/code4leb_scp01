# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Being respectful and constructive
- Accepting feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, insulting comments, and personal attacks.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, and contributions that violate these standards.

## Reporting
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please open an issue with relevant details for maintainers to review.
